**Does Kejriwal need security at all?**

The answer may be "No" by a good number of people today. Answer it again going back in pre-2012 era. Just 3 years back from today, no one would have expected an unarmed common man exposing Robert Vadra, Mukesh Ambani and alikes. It was a routine those days to read about murder of one or the other RTI activist or a whistle-blower who would have tried to expose some local politician or a goon. Forget about an Aam Aadmi, the most powerful media never dared to have a story on these people. The day Kejriwal spoke against Robert Vadra a lot of people would have said "अब इसका बचना मुश्किल है" Kejriwal had a very serious threat to his life much more than many other politician in India.

**What did we do to convince him?**

He must be admired to have heard the same thing thousands of times. I am sure 90 out of 100 people he would be meeting those days would have requested him for the same. Hundreds of groups would have sent him requests. A signature campaign was started on causes.com. 10800 people signed in support. Accompanied by my friend Prashant Kumar from Jaipur, I went to Kaushambhi office. Dr. Vipin Mittal, a common friend of Arvind and me was with us. We handed over the list of singatures to him. His simple response was that signature smile. I requested him to answer on camera as I had promised the signatories that I would upload Arvind's response on youtube.
